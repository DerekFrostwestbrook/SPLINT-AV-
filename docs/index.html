A clearer second opinion.

SPLINT MAX is an on-demand scanner that helps you inspect selected files and startup locations, understand what was flagged, and choose what to do next. The FREE and PRO editions are intentionally much simpler than MAX.

A straightforward workflow

Scan. Review. Decide.

01 / SELECT

Choose what to check

Pick a quick scan, all available drives, or a folder you select.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Read each finding

Review the indicator and report. Heuristic signals can have false positives.

03 / TAKE ACTION

Choose what happens next

Leave an item alone or explicitly select an eligible finding for quarantine.

!
A second opinion, not real-time antivirus protection.

Detections can be wrong or incomplete. Keep Windows and your primary antivirus enabled, and review findings before taking action.
